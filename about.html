About Animal Insight

Thank you for visiting!

Animal Insight was created in 2026 as a place to share my thoughts, research, and perspectives on animal rights and animal welfare. The topics I write about are varied, exploring subjects such as the food industry, veganism and vegetarianism, nutrition, social justice, religion, philosophy, and politics.

Where relevant, articles include links to the statistics, studies, and other sources referenced throughout the post, as well as links to additional information for readers who would like to explore a particular subject further.

About The Author

I believe the animal industries lack transparency and honesty, and I've previously tried to address this through various forms such as video content creation, street activism, and satirical social media pages.

Hopefully Animal Insight can continue that work in some way, and teach at least one person something they didn't already know about animals or veganism. 💚
